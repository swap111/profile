Founder · Artist · Teacher

Damruki

School of Rhythm and Seven Swaras (formerly Rhythm Seven)

Damruki teaches Indian classical music, both Hindustani and Carnatic, and percussion to children and adults from many cultures and professions. It treats music as a language, percussion as both science and art, and practice as a path to Nada Yoga, the union of sound and consciousness.

  • Percussion: Tabla and Mridangam, plus the instrument that best suits each learner's style and repertoire
  • Styles explored: Khayal, Dhrupad, Prabandh Gayaki, Bhajan, Kirtan, Ghazal, Qawwali, folk, and accompaniment for dance
  • Personal learning paths: a taalim and practice routine for each student, with workshops, concerts and performance tours
  • Music for well-being: rhythmic cycles, melody and improvisation used for calm, focus and personal growth
  • Community: Kavi Sammelans, festivals, TV features such as the Thyagaraja–Purandaradasa Aradhana, and the Bansuri Beats series

Suphala Care

Holistic Nutrition & Wellness Solutions

Suphala Care is the nutrition and wellness practice of Dr Suguna Sapre, a nutritionist with a PhD in renal nutrition. It builds personal food plans around each family's needs, tastes and traditional Indian kitchen. I build and run its digital platform, from the website and online booking to programmes and the SuViva network, and help shape its growth.

  • Who it helps: healthy weight, women's health at every stage, kids and toddlers, senior citizens
  • Chronic conditions: diabetes, heart health, hypertension and kidney care
  • How it works: health assessment, then a personal plan, holistic guidance on activity, sleep and stress, and regular follow-ups
  • SuViva: a network of complementary practitioners (yoga, Ayurveda, counselling, physiotherapy, lactation) plus free wellness tools. Suphala Care plans the food; SuViva supports everything around it.
